# Sound credits

Every sound here is by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released as Creative Commons Zero (CC0, public domain). Credit is not required, but here it is.
Each sound comes as `.ogg` (the original) and `.m4a` (AAC, for Safari before version 17). `js/ui/sound.js` plays them with Howler.js.
